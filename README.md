One word for the boss: **Hovercraft**. The boss who hovers over your shoulder, drifting from desk to desk — a real word with a second meaning that lands on its own, in any workplace. The program scores a shortlist of boss words against the judge's five criteria (a single word people actually use, funny on its own, true about a real boss habit, fresh, and travelling across industries) and writes the winner to `/output/entry.txt`. Hovercraft wins the shortlist at 53 points: Micromanager is true but the first word anyone reaches for, Clockwatcher is familiar and flat, and Delegator is too neutral to be funny.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

It writes the single word to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`).
